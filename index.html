How "Tier 2" is defined — and why it matters

The term "Tier 2" is used differently depending on the framework. This model uses the TSMC-centric definition from our supply chain data, but the distinction matters for interpreting severity:

Framework "Tier 2" means Source
TSMC Supply Chain (used here) Supplier to a TSMC Tier 1 (e.g., supplies Unimicron, not TSMC directly), or smaller substrate maker not in the 3D Fabric Alliance TSMC_Suppliers.csv
Market Share (Intel Market Research) Companies outside the top 5 by FC-BGA production value ABF Substrate Market Outlook
Automotive Supply Chain (IATF 16949) Supplier to an automotive Tier 1 (Continental, Bosch, ZF), not the OEM directly Industry standard
Substrate Industry (Yole/TechInsights) Mid-sized producers below Unimicron/Ibiden/Shinko class Analyst convention
Key ambiguity: Nan Ya PCB is "Tier 2" in the TSMC framework but holds 13.5% global ABF market share (#3 worldwide). If Nan Ya goes offline, 13.5% of global ABF supply disappears — that is a major event, not a minor T2 disruption. Conversely, Subtron (merged into Unimicron, makes SiP substrates) going offline is a niche impact.
